  • True: the support platform lowers away from the spherical payload and the strip then creeps slowly downward under the sustained load; the payload centre sits 6.66 mm lower at the last frame than at the intervention, measured on the decoded pixels.
  • Counterfactual: the same platform lowering happens, and the strip then reverses its creep and lifts the same payload by 25.66 mm; the payload is visibly higher than in True at the last frame and the lift is a sustained rise, not a release oscillation, even though gravity still acts on it.

  • Geometry holds in every frame of both branches: the strip stays attached to the upper clamp, the payload stays suspended and clear of the platform, and nothing floats, interpenetrates or leaves the frame.
